PLACEHOLDER: App Icon
===================

សម្រាប់ production build, សូមដាក់ app icon នៅទីនេះ:
- Windows: icon.png (256x256 or larger)
- Mac: icon.png (512x512 or larger) or icon.icns
- Linux: icon.png (512x512 or larger)

អ្នកអាចបង្កើត icon ដោយប្រើ:
- https://icon.kitchen/
- Photoshop / GIMP
- Online tools

Icon Requirements:
- Format: PNG (transparent background)
- Size: 512x512px (recommended)
- Content: ⚡ Lightning bolt + "EZA PRO" text
